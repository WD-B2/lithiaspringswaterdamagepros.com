Basement water in Lithia Springs, GA: what to do right now

Our emergency line takes basement calls around the clock. While you wait, stop the source if it is a pipe, lift boxes and electronics off the floor, and keep people and pets out of the water. Do not go down the stairs if water is near outlets or the panel.

Call (943) 469-0138 once you are safe. A dispatcher will ask where the water entered and how deep it is, so crews can bring the right pumps and extraction units (see water extraction). Standing water that is only a day old is far easier to recover from than water that has soaked drywall and carpet for a week.

Electrical and gas risk in a flooded basement

Water and electricity do not mix, and basements often hold the panel, the furnace and the water heater. Stay out of standing water if you see it touching outlets, appliances or wiring. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it without stepping through water.

If you smell gas, see sparks or are unsure about the panel, leave the house and call 911 or the utility before you call the restoration line. A dry basement can be rebuilt. A person cannot.

What is and is not known about basements here

The research behind this page found no source that documents how many Lithia Springs homes have basements, so we will not claim they are common. Many local homes may sit on slabs or crawl spaces instead. Check your own house, because this page applies only to a below-grade room, a walkout level or a lower floor cut into a slope.

What is documented is the creek. Douglas County describes Woodrow Wilson Park on Mt. Vernon Road as lying in the Sweetwater Creek 100-year floodplain and prone to flooding with no advance notice.

In 2009, the creek basin reached the 500-year flood level according to the National Weather Service. If you live near the creek, check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask the county about flood history for your lot.

Where it comes from

Four ways water gets into a below-grade room

Sump pump failure

Pumps stop when the power goes out, a float sticks or a switch fails. Heavy rain can also outrun an undersized pump.

Seepage through walls and floors

Saturated ground pushes water through cracks and the joint where the wall meets the slab. It tends to appear after long rains.

Burst or leaking plumbing

Supply lines, water heaters and washer hoses often live in the basement, so a failure there floods the lowest room first.

Surface water through windows and doors

Window wells fill, stairwell drains clog and water pours in at grade. Flood water carries sediment and may be contaminated.

What happens once crews reach a flooded basement

  1. Pump and extract

    Submersible pumps move deep water out, then extraction units with wands clear what is left on floors and carpet.

  2. Remove what cannot be dried

    Wet carpet pad, soaked insulation and the lowest part of drywall are cut out because they hold water and feed bacteria.

  3. Dry the structure

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until moisture meters show the concrete, framing and remaining drywall at a dry reference.

  4. Clean and treat surfaces

    Hard surfaces are cleaned with disinfectant, and HEPA air scrubbers can run when the water was dirty or when there is dust from cuts.

Finished basements: contents, carpet and drywall

A finished basement holds furniture, flooring, drywall and often a second living room. Contents are moved first, with hard goods wiped and dried and soft goods judged by what the water carried. Paper, photos and electronics should be set aside separately.

Drywall in a basement often sits against a concrete wall, so moisture can stay trapped between them. That is why a flood cut and open cavity matter more here than upstairs. See our guide on wall and drywall water damage repair for the cutting process in detail.

Restoration is not waterproofing

Cleaning up a basement removes water and dries what remains. It does not stop water from coming back. If your basement flooded because of a crack, a poor grade or a missing drain, that cause has to be fixed by a waterproofing or drainage contractor, and the repair is a separate job.

Ask questions about the pump, the grading outside and gutter discharge. A restoration crew can point out what it sees, but we do not promise that water will never return.

What shapes the cost of a basement cleanup

  • Depth and volume

    Several inches of water takes a different pump setup than an inch of seepage.

  • Water category

    Contaminated water means more removal and disinfection.

  • Finishes and contents

    A finished basement with carpet, drywall and furniture is a larger scope than a bare concrete room.

  • Drying days

    Concrete holds moisture for a long time, which adds equipment days.

  • Hours of the call

    Emergency dispatch overnight is a different logistics job from a scheduled morning visit.

Common Questions

Do you handle basement water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Lithia Springs?

Yes. Dispatch answers at night, and a sump pump that quits in a storm is the kind of call it handles most. Crews are sent on weekends too, with same-day response when available. Call (943) 469-0138 and say whether the water is still rising.

Is a basement common in Lithia Springs homes?

The research does not document it, so we will not say it is. Many homes may have slabs or crawl spaces. If yours has a below-grade room, the mechanisms here still apply.

My sump pump failed. What now?

Check the power and the float, but do not wade through water to reach the pump. If it is dead and water is rising, call (943) 469-0138 and begin lifting contents. A replacement pump is a plumber's job.

Is basement water always dirty?

No, but you cannot tell by looking. Water from a supply line starts clean. Groundwater, creek overflow and sewer water are contaminated, so treat unknown water as dirty until a crew sorts it.

Can you stop the basement from flooding again?

No. Restoration cleans and dries the space. Waterproofing, grading and drainage work are separate jobs. You can see how we cover neighboring areas on the Austell service page.

Why do my walls feel damp after the water was removed?

Concrete and drywall hold moisture long after the standing water is gone. Drying equipment runs until readings show the structure is dry, and that can take several days.
