Where crews work out of Lithia Springs

Lithia Springs is an unincorporated community in Douglas County, and ZIP 30122 is home base. From there, crews run along I-20 and US 78 into the surrounding Douglas and Cobb County communities. Towns with their own pages

Each page below covers local streets, creeks, ZIP codes and the kinds of water problems that show up there.

Austell

An incorporated city about 4 miles from Lithia Springs, mostly in Cobb County with ZIPs 30106 and 30168. It sits on Sweetwater Creek. See water damage restoration in Austell.

Douglasville

The Douglas County seat, with street ZIPs 30134 and 30135. Its mix of older downtown buildings and apartments shapes the calls. See water damage restoration in Douglasville.

Mableton

Cobb County's newest city, where voters approved cityhood in November 2022, served by ZIP 30126. Nickajack Creek runs through it. See water damage restoration in Mableton.

Lithia Springs

The home community, ZIP 30122, covered on the home page, with Sweetwater Creek State Park and Historic Lithia Springs among its landmarks.

Why coverage is explained this way

Municipal lines, county lines and ZIP codes overlap around Lithia Springs. Austell straddles Cobb and Douglas counties, Mableton became a city in 2022 with boundaries that cut across post office areas, and Hiram sits in a different county altogether. A call settles what a map cannot.

Different jurisdictions also mean different permit offices. Douglas County Building Permits and Inspections covers Lithia Springs, while Austell, Douglasville and Mableton each run their own building offices. Ask yours before a repair that needs a permit.

Nearby places crews also reach

Powder Springs (ZIP 30127) is a Cobb County city a few miles northwest of Austell. Hiram (ZIP 30141) lies in Paulding County, not Cobb or Douglas, so confirm coverage by phone. Winston (ZIP 30187) is an unincorporated community in Douglas County, farther west than the other places listed here.

Marietta, Clarkdale, Union City and Fairburn are also within reach. Clarkdale is the historic mill village area of Austell, and it carries a National Register historic district, which matters if your home is an older mill-era house with original plumbing. None of these places has its own page, so describe your address on the call.
