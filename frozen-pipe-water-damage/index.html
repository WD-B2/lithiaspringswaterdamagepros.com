The thaw is when the leak starts

A pipe that froze overnight can look fine until the morning warms up. Then water appears at the split, often inside a wall, and it is an emergency from that moment. Our line is open 24/7, so call at any hour.

While you wait, close the main water valve, open the faucet nearest the damage, lift belongings off wet floors and keep everyone clear of ceilings that sag. This page is about frozen pipe water damage in Lithia Springs, GA, specifically the ice blockage case, which differs from an ordinary burst supply line.

Drying after a split pipe follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether its crews work to it.

What actually splits the pipe

Water expands by about nine percent as it turns to ice. That alone rarely cracks a pipe, because the ice plug has room to push. The damage usually comes from the water trapped between the plug and a closed faucet, which has nowhere to go and builds pressure until the weakest section gives.

So the split is often not where the ice sits. It is on the downstream side, and it may stay sealed by the ice itself until the plug melts. That is why the flood arrives with the thaw, not with the freeze.

How often Lithia Springs gets a hard freeze

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals for Atlanta Hartsfield, the nearest long-record station and roughly 20 to 25 miles east, show an average of 35.7 days a year with a low of 32 degrees Fahrenheit or colder. Lithia Springs-specific normals were not checked.

Many Georgia homes are built without freeze protection because cold snaps are short, so a few bad nights can catch pipes unprepared. Know where your main valve is before January, and insulate exposed lines in crawl spaces, garages and exterior walls.

Where pipes freeze in Georgia homes

Pipes freeze where heat does not reach them and cold air does.

Crawl spaces

Vents and gaps let cold air across supply lines that hang in an unheated void.

Exterior walls

A line run along a north wall with thin insulation sits close to the outside temperature.

Garages and utility rooms

Washer hookups and water heater lines in unheated rooms are common trouble spots.

Hose bibs

A hose left attached holds water in the spigot and the pipe behind it.

Never use open flame to thaw a pipe

Torches, kerosene heaters and open flames near framing start fires and can burn through a pipe joint. Use a hair dryer or warm towels, starting at the faucet end and moving toward the ice.

If water has reached outlets or the panel, keep the power off, stay out of the wet area and call 911 or the utility before the restoration line.

What a crew does after a thaw leak

  1. Find the split

    Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera trace the wet path, which often runs well past the visible stain.

  2. Remove the water

    Pumps and extraction units clear standing water from floors and low points.

  3. Open the cavity

    Drywall and insulation that are saturated come out so the wall can dry. Wet drywall is cut back to dry material.

  4. Dry and recheck

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run until readings match a dry reference, a process described on the structural drying page.

Thaw slowly or call first

If a pipe is only frozen and not yet leaking, thawing it slowly and watching for drips is reasonable. If you find a split, the next step is shutoff, not repair. A plumber fixes the pipe, and restoration crews dry what got wet.

Ceilings below an upstairs break are common casualties, and the ceiling repair page explains when a ceiling can stay and when it has to come down.

What shapes the price after a freeze

  • Hidden spread

    Water inside wall cavities takes more opening and drying than a surface spill.

  • Hours of leakage

    A split caught during the thaw costs less than one that ran while no one was home.

  • Insulation and finishes

    Wet batts and drywall are replaced, while sealed wood may dry in place.

  • Equipment days

    Thick framing and cold weather slow drying and extend rental time.

  • Rebuild

    Repair of drywall, paint and trim comes after the building tests dry.

Common Questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Lithia Springs?

Yes. The worst of a thaw often shows up overnight when the pipe lets go, so the line is answered then too. Weekend calls are dispatched the same way, with same-day response when available. Call (943) 469-0138 as soon as you see water and close the main valve while you wait.

Are frozen pipes common in Lithia Springs?

Not as common as in colder states. The nearest NOAA normals show 35.7 freezing days a year, so the risk is real but short, which is why exposed lines get caught off guard.

Can a pipe be split without leaking yet?

Yes. Ice can hold a split closed until it melts, so a pipe that thawed an hour ago may begin to leak this afternoon. Watch walls and ceilings after a hard freeze.

Should I leave a faucet dripping?

A slow drip on a vulnerable line helps because moving water relieves pressure and resists freezing. It is a stopgap, not a fix for poorly insulated pipes.

Is frozen pipe damage covered by insurance?

Sudden and accidental bursts are often covered, but conditions such as a vacant house with the heat off may change that. Read your policy and ask your insurer what applies.
